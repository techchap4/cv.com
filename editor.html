02 · SUMMARY

Professional summary

In 3–4 sentences, cover who you are, what you do, your years of experience, and your top 2 skills.

03 · EXPERIENCE

Work experience

Add your roles in reverse chronological order. For each job, include the job title, company name, location, and start–end dates (or "Present"), plus 3–4 bullet points on what you achieved — not just your duties. Use numbers where you can, e.g. "Built 5 websites for local clients."

04 · EDUCATION

Education

For each school, add the degree or certificate, school name, year (e.g. 2021 – 2024), and location.

05 · SKILLS

Skills

Separate skills with commas. Templates automatically style them.

06 · PROJECTS

Projects

Especially important for developers. For each project, add a project name, a link, the technologies used, and a 1-line description.

07 · EXTRAS

Additional information

Optional content that still adds value. Used by templates such as academic and executive layouts.
